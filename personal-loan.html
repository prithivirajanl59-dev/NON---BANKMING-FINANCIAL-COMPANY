Frequently Asked Questions

What can I use the personal loan for?

You can use the funds for any personal purpose, such as a vacation, wedding, medical expenses, or home renovation. There are no restrictions on how you use the money.

How is the interest rate determined?

The interest rate depends on your credit score, income, employment type, and the loan amount and tenure you choose. A higher credit score can get you a lower interest rate.

What is the minimum and maximum loan amount?

The minimum loan amount is typically ₹50,000, and the maximum is up to ₹20 Lakhs, based on your eligibility.
