Frequently Asked Questions

How do I redeem my reward points?

You can redeem your reward points through the Global Trust Bank's mobile app or internet banking portal. The options include redeeming for cashback, booking flights, or purchasing products from our rewards catalog.

What are the charges for cash withdrawals?

Cash withdrawals from an ATM using your credit card are subject to a high interest rate from the day of withdrawal and a transaction fee. It's generally not recommended.

How is my credit limit determined?

Your credit limit is determined based on several factors, including your income, employment history, and credit score. A higher income and a good credit score generally lead to a higher credit limit.

Note: Interest rates and credit limits are subject to change based on bank policies and individual credit assessment. Final approval and card terms are at the sole discretion of Global Trust Bank.
